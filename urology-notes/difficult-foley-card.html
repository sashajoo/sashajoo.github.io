Common call

The Foley that will not go in

Page

RN: Mr. X, bed 12, 74, in retention. Two tries, the Foley will not pass. Can urology come?

Danger signs

  • Blood at the meatus or pelvic trauma: possible urethral injury. No blind attempt.
  • Catheter kinks and bloody discharge after tries: possible urethral perforation. Call your senior.
  • Retention that nobody can drain is urgent. Do not keep trying again and again.

Key decision

Ifblood at the meatus, or recent prostatectomy or urethral surgery → stop; call your senior.
Ifthe coudé passes to the hub and urine flows → inflate the balloon slowly.
Ifthe coudé fails → stop; call your senior (12 Fr silicone, guidewire or cystoscopy).

Call the senior

S
Urology, about bed 12. Cannot place a Foley in Mr. X, 74, in retention.
B
Prior TURP. Two 16 Fr straight tries, then one gentle coudé try; no blood.
A
Bladder full and tender, vitals stable; maybe bladder neck contracture or stricture.
R
Please come for a 12 Fr silicone, guidewire or flexible cystoscopy.

RN, registered nurse; Fr, French, the catheter size unit; TURP, transurethral resection of the prostate.
