Testicular torsion

Page

ED: Mr. L., 19, sudden left testicular pain for 3 hours, vomited twice. The ED asks for a urology consult.

Danger signs

  • Sudden one-sided scrotal pain, sometimes with nausea or vomiting, suggests torsion.
  • Torsion is a surgical emergency. The chance of saving the testis drops after 6 hours of pain.
  • Testes saved, by hours of pain: 97% at 0 to 6 hours, 79% at 7 to 12 hours, 61% at 13 to 18 hours, 43% at 19 to 24 hours, 24% at 25 to 48 hours, 7% over 48 hours.
  • Pain felt only in the lower abdomen or groin can still be torsion. Examine the scrotum.

Key decision

Ifsuspicion is high (sudden pain, hard or high testis, vomiting) → call your senior now. OR without ultrasound.
Ifsuspicion is low or unclear → urgent Doppler, then decide with your senior.
Ifthe OR is delayed and pain is under 6 hours → manual detorsion with your senior varies by site, followed by surgery.
