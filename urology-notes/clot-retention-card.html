Hematuria and clot retention

Page

RN: Mr. P., POD 0 after TURP, on CBI. Urine is dark red, the bag stopped filling, and he has severe suprapubic pain.

Danger signs

  • HR up, BP down, Hgb falling, cold extremities. HR often rises before BP falls.
  • Tense abdomen or shoulder pain, with low BP and bradycardia.
  • New confusion or nausea, or fever.

Key decision

Ifthe op note shows a known or suspected perforation → no hand irrigation until your senior decides varies by site.
Ifhand irrigation clears it and the bag drains → restart CBI, slow it to light pink.
Ifit will not clear, or HR is up and BP is down → call your senior now for a catheter position check, a new 3-way catheter and traction. The OR if these fail.
